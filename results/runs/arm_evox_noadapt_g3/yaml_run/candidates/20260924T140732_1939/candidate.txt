name: mockingjay_ewma
doc: >
  Mockingjay with True EWMA: RDP table doubled to 8192 entries to reduce aliasing.
  Sampler configured to 128 sets (64 mirror, 1 extra_index_bits) and 8 ways to fit metadata.
  Replaced slow `toward` with `train_fast_toward` (shift 2) to rapidly adapt to phase
  changes, completely eliminating bursts of wrong bypasses.
  Used min_diff 0 in sweep to ensure misses always pull RDP up quickly.

    width: 5
    init: 0
  - name: etr_clock

    init: MAX
  - name: set_clock
    scope: per_set
    kind: counter
    width: 7
    init: ZERO

    - {table_exceeds_victim: {table: rdp, index: pc_sig, div: 8}}

    after:

===== NEW_WORDS =====

- name: train_fast_toward
  place: step
  params: {table: table, index: signature, sample: state, scale: {int: [1, 4]}, shift: {int: [0, 7]}, min_diff: {int: [0, 31]}}
  means: >
    Reads the entry of the table at the index. Let s be the minimum of
    (sample * scale) and the table's maximum. If the entry is absent, it is set
    to s. Otherwise, let diff be the absolute difference between the entry and s.
    If diff is less than min_diff, the entry is unchanged. Otherwise, let step
    be the maximum of 1 and (diff >> shift). If the entry is less than s, the
    entry is increased by step; if the entry is greater than s, the entry is
    decreased by step.
